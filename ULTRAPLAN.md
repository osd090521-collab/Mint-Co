## 1. The model in one paragraph

Mint & Co sells **digital presence as a monthly subscription** to local service businesses.
The wedge is a £30 one-time Google review stand — cheap enough to sell on a cold walk-in,
valuable enough to prove we're real. Every stand customer is a warm lead for one of three
monthly packages (£49 / £119 / £179). No setup fees, no minimum term, cancel anytime —
which keeps our "no pressure, no surprises" brand promise intact — and a fair buyout
option protects us on the website asset. Revenue compounds: every closed client is
recurring income, not a one-off project.

## 3. The three packages

All monthly, rolling, cancel anytime. No setup fee, no minimum term.
Review stand included free in every package.

### Essentials — "Get found & trusted" — £49/mo
The full Reputation category plus a managed Google presence. For businesses not
ready for a website but bleeding customers on Google.
- Google review stand + funnel (stand free)
- Staff incentive system + leaderboard
- Review dashboard + monthly report + review response drafting
- Google Business Profile setup, then managed monthly
- WhatsApp & social links tidy-up
- **Market anchor:** GBP management alone sells at £50–£400/mo; review software alone at £60–£320/mo. Essentials undercuts either component bought separately — at £49/mo, it undercuts the low end of both.

### Growth — "Get found, trusted & chosen" — £119/mo
The core offer. Everything in Essentials, plus the full build:
- Full website: designed, built, copywritten, hosted, secured, backed up
- Website edits included (fair use, ~1 hr/mo)
- Local SEO foundations (Search Console, citations, map)
- Business email setup
- Booking system setup
- Analytics in the monthly report
- **Market anchor:** agency websites alone run £1,200–£8,000 one-time plus £29–£100/mo care; entry digital-marketing retainers start at £500–£825/mo. £119/mo with zero upfront undercuts both substantially — this is land-grab pricing, not the long-run target (see §7 and the 24 Jul amendment above).

### Complete — "The full digital presence" — £179/mo
Everything in Growth, plus the visibility and content engine:
- **AI search optimisation (AEO/GEO) — exclusive to Complete.** Schema, entity
  signals, answer-format pages: the business AI assistants recommend
- Local SEO management (not just foundations)
- Social media setup + monthly branded post templates
- Monthly local answer page (content that compounds)
- Branding polish at build
- Priority support
- **Market anchor:** AI search optimisation alone sells at £299–£1,500+/mo
  (Yellowball from £1,500/mo); local SEO management alone at £300–£1,500/mo.
  Complete is cheaper than either single line item, and no one else offers
  AEO to local businesses at this price — at £179/mo the gap is even larger.

### Terms (apply to all packages)
- **Rolling monthly, cancel anytime.** No setup fee, no minimum term. This is the trust
  differentiator — every competitor locks clients in; we don't need to because the
  monthly report proves value every month.
- **Website buyout on cancellation:** the site is ours while subscribed. On cancel,
  the client may buy the site outright for **£595** (full export, domain and GBP were
  always theirs). Otherwise the site comes down after a 30-day grace period.
  This is honest, protects the build investment, and stays true to
  "you own what's yours" — domain and Google profile are never hostages.
- **Billing:** Stripe subscriptions, card on file, invoice receipts. First month billed
  at go-live, not at signature (we carry the build; another trust lever).

## 4. The £30 review stand — the wedge

**What it is:** an NFC + QR stand configured to the business's Google review link,
plus a printed instruction card. One-time £30. No subscription, no automation, no catch.

**Why it exists:** it converts a cold walk-in into a transaction in five minutes.
A business that has paid us £30 and watched reviews come in that same week takes the
package call. It's also self-qualifying — an owner who won't spend £30 on their own
reputation will never buy a £249/mo package.

**Unit economics:** blank NFC stands source at roughly £5–8/unit; comparable
retail stands sell for £25–£100+ with no service behind them. At £30 configured and
delivered in person, margin is ~£20 and the sales conversation is the real product.

**The upsell line:** "The stand gets you reviews. The packages make sure people
actually find them — and you."

## 5. The staff incentive system

**Principles (locked):**
1. **Owner-funded.** Rewards never come out of Mint & Co's pocket. We supply the
   system; the owner supplies the prize. This is a feature, not a limitation — the
   owner picks rewards their team actually wants.
2. **Standardised.** One mechanic, one template, every business. Zero redesign per client.
3. **Fast setup.** Under 30 minutes on-site, no software for staff to learn.

**The mechanic — "Name in the review":**
Staff ask happy customers to *mention their first name* in the Google review
("Ask them to pop your name in the review"). Names in review text are countable
straight off the business's public review page — no automation, no app, no tracking
hardware. Each month we tally names into a leaderboard; the owner rewards the winner.

**Why this mechanic:** it's the only incentive mechanism that needs zero extra tech,
is fully auditable by the owner (the reviews are public), rewards genuine service
(customers don't name staff who were mediocre), and complies with Google's rules —
staff are rewarded for *asking*, customers are never paid for reviewing.

**The copy-paste deployment kit (build once, reuse forever):**
| Item | Format |
|------|--------|
| Staff briefing card — "how to ask" script, 3 lines | Printed card, brand template |
| Break-room poster — current month leaderboard | A4 template, name slots |
| Owner's one-pager — how the system works, suggested reward menu | PDF |
| Leaderboard tracker — auto-tally sheet, month tabs | Google Sheet template, copy per client |
| Monthly report — reviews gained, rating trend, staff leaderboard | Brand template, emailed |

**Suggested reward menu for owners (they choose, they fund):** £25 cash bonus ·
an hour off, paid · free product/service · winner's choice under £30.
Guidance: reward should feel instant and visible — announced the day the month closes.

**Instant-reward layer:** alongside the monthly winner, owners are encouraged to give
an on-the-spot micro-reward (verbal shout-out in the group chat + a coffee/soft drink)
the first time any staff member's name appears in a review each month. Instant
reinforcement drives the habit; the monthly prize drives the competition.

## 6. Sales motion

**Funnel:** Walk-in / DM → £30 stand sale → reviews arrive within days →
follow-up visit with their numbers ("you got 9 reviews in 2 weeks — here's what
happens when people can also find your website") → package close → monthly report
sustains retention and referrals.

**Channels, in order:**
1. **In-person walk-ins** with a demo stand in hand (beachhead: barbers in Pinner/Harrow — David's lead sheet)
2. **Free website audit** (existing site CTA) — feeds Growth/Complete directly
3. **Instagram @bymintco** — before/after content, review-count screenshots
4. **Referrals** — one free month for any referral that converts (costs us margin, not cash)

**Rules of engagement (unchanged):** site live and polished before cold outreach ·
no fabricated testimonials ever · studio voice, no individual names in public copy ·
positioning is any under-served local business, barbers are just the first door.

## 7. Unit economics & projections

**Cost per client (monthly, approximate):** Framer hosting ~£15–20 (Growth/Complete) ·
Stripe fees ~1.5% + 20p · our time: ~1–2 hrs/mo steady-state (report, edits, GBP posts).
Essentials carries near-zero hard cost.

| Book of clients (mix: 20% E / 55% G / 25% C, avg ~£120 at the reverted 24 Jul prices) | MRR | Annualised |
|---|---|---|
| 10 clients | ~£1,200 | ~£14,400 |
| 25 clients | ~£3,000 | ~£36,000 |
| 50 clients | ~£6,000 | ~£72,000 |

*(At the 16 Jul prices — £99/£249/£449, avg ~£269 — the same book runs roughly 2.2×
higher. That comparison is the real cost of the 24 Jul reversion; see the amendment
banner at the top of this document.)*

Plus stand sales (pure margin, ~£20/unit) and buyouts (£595 each) as irregular income.

**Capacity check:** steady-state service load at 25 clients ≈ 25–50 hrs/mo across the
team — sustainable alongside new builds. Beyond ~40 clients, template/automation
investment (report generation, GBP posting) becomes the priority, not headcount.

**Churn defence:** the monthly report is the retention product — every month the
client sees reviews gained, rating trend, and their staff leaderboard. Cancel-anytime
converts to loyalty only if value is *visible* monthly. Target churn <3%/mo.

## 10. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Cancel-anytime → early churn after build | Buyout (£595) recovers build cost; bill from go-live; report makes value visible monthly |
| £30 stand buyers never upgrade | Fine — stand is profitable alone; follow-up visit with their own numbers is the conversion moment |
| Incentive system gamed (fake name-drops) | Reviews are public and owner-audited; Google's own spam filters; we tally only genuine reviews |
| Google policy on incentivised reviews | We never reward *customers* — only staff, for asking. Kit language keeps this line bright |
| Service load outgrows team | Template-first ops from day one; automation phase gated at 40 clients |
| Underpricing (we're far below market) | Intentional for land-grab; prices reviewable per cohort — existing clients keep their rate (loyalty lever) |
